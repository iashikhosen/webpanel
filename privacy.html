WebPanel Privacy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Data stored by WebPanel

WebPanel stores your saved tab names, URLs, pin states, and panel preferences locally in Chrome using chrome.storage.local. This information is used only to provide your saved workspace and is not sent by WebPanel to the developer.

Optional future-update form

If you choose to use the optional “Get future tool updates” form, the email address and any information you submit are sent directly to Google Forms and the form owner. WebPanel does not read, transmit, or store those form responses.

Third-party websites

WebPanel displays websites you choose in its side panel. Those websites operate under their own privacy policies and may collect information directly from you.
